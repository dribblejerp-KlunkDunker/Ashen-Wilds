# Evacuation Complications

Structured consolidation of ten proposed complications. They test promises when inconvenient, not guarantee an ambush. Exposure grows once old shelter is dismantled. Use three core complications, others only as relevant alternatives/consequences. Earlier repairs and explicit agreements must matter. Revised 2026-11-05 for owner decision O1 (only humans are people): the Gutter Kin are a human household of road waifs. The original is in git history at commit fd60061.

## 1. Garran Wold arrives

Proposed name for injured earlier salvage worker, not core four Hands. Stiff leg, borrowed footwear, hears Kin may leave. Wants to know who settled his loss without him, usable boots, and Iss looking at him. He calls the Kin gutter thieves; the theft was real, the word is older than it. Oren/Hesh’s agreement does not settle Garran’s claim. Alternatives: replacement, bounded accepted compensation, explicit unresolved claim without control over Sere’s movement. Anger may remain after settlement.

## 2. Elsin finds an estate mark

Repaired fitting among Sere’s tools: Elsin claims continuing ownership; Sere cites abandonment and labor. Rauth may begin removing other bundles without orders. Oren can enforce limits; substitute, allowance, deferred dispute, or evidence can resolve. No surprise rule voiding the whole agreement for one mark.

## 3. Brik challenges actual load

Iss’s bundle, bedding, and container each seem small but combine beyond agreement. Need fewer goods, another load/carrier, rearrangement, or more time. Brik admits knife promised for Iss’s private bundle. He is both exploited and responsible for concealing capacity already counted on by everyone. Practical solution need not heal resentment.

## 4. Kett admits route damage

Old fallback collapsed; inspected main route may still work. Concealment makes Hesh less tolerant of court risk and Marda distrust information. Inspect/update fallback or accept absence explicitly. Lie already damages coordination; no unrelated hidden collapse is needed.

## 5. A southern message arrives

Requests fuller inventory before more material leaves, not explicitly a broken evacuation. Elsin reads broadly, Oren interprets, Rauth prefers delay. Kin already dismantling shelter bear cost. Tests whether Oren keeps promises when superior’s wishes inconvenience him. Destroying letter does not erase instruction.

## 6. Moth retains final screen

Wants shelter intact until frame ready, route open, inspection largely complete, Sere ready. Rational sequencing, not obstruction. Let Moth remove it herself when movement real.

## 7. Garden Hands need same gear

Pantry lintel shifts while frame prepared. People can evacuate; property may be lost. Vey initially assumes trapped people; Marda asks what is true. Clear room, accept loss, limited intervention, alternative brace, separate Dema crew. Earlier proper bracing can prevent complication; do not erase earned improvements.

## 8. Weather narrows working window

Ash/wind impair visibility and shouted instructions, not instant arbitrary death. Sere coughs in the ash, and Remnant and crew alike step back from the cough before asking what it is. Pressures departure deadline, safe work, guards, Sere’s pace, route confidence. Sheltered stages, communication, lighter load, inspected pause can work. Prudent halt is not automatic failure.

## 9. Sere withdraws consent

Pain, missing tool, destination concern, or fundamental refusal. Hesh agreed but cannot make her freight. Address concrete issue, rest, correct misrepresentation. If refusal absolute, others must decide their own departure, not become automatically bound to remain. No single persuasion roll concealing what she actually wants.

## 10. Grey Lash reaches limits

Longer final approach and extra load trigger Marda’s boundary. Hesh hears abandonment, Vey promises, Tern asks whose hands. Specified destination/load/contingencies help but do not erase exhaustion. Reduce burden, relief, or explicitly accepted narrower completion.

## Preferred main version

Garran exposes unresolved harm; Brik forces honest distribution; Elsin’s fitting forces Oren to restrain his side. Responsibility crosses groups. Weather/messages/structural crises are variations, not a required disaster parade. All six may reach lodge while Garran remains angry, Brik resents Iss, Elsin preserves claim, and Oren resents restraining Rauth. Success need not mean agreement or forgiveness.
