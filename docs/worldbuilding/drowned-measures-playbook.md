# Drowned Measures: Player Forecasting and Frontier Playbook

Prepared 2026-11-09. Proposed lore and custom d20 tabletop/design rules, not implemented game runtime or finalized canon. Additive companion to silt-readers-and-drowned-measures.md and the quarry package. Consolidates the conversation's Cutwardens, regional events, Rootstone Witness and failed-forecast quest, then adds a usable player procedure and an orchard-specific event table. Structured consolidation, not verbatim transcript. No existing files, code, maps, assets, saves or checkpoints are replaced.

## 1. Player mechanic: Read the Ground

Purpose: spend time and gather evidence to choose a less exposed route or task. Forecasts do not change the underlying water, reveal hidden predators, certify drinking water or supply prophecy. These are fictional rules, not real-world safety advice.

### Forecast card

Record: location and route; intended party/load; question; reference; current observation; comparison evidence; start time and review time; observed facts; expected change; unknowns; withdrawal triggers; source/witness; status. Status is incomplete, provisional, supported, review due or withdrawn. Supported means justified by current evidence, not guaranteed correct.

Ask one bounded question: 'Can we reasonably plan a foot return along the orchard wall during this salvage trip?' Not 'Is the basin safe?' A materially changed load, task or route requires review.

### Field actions and costs

Use game minutes for this tabletop prototype only. These values are not changes to the browser campaign clock. Director states exposure and access before commitment.

| Action | Default time | Result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Read a notice or question a witness | 5 minutes | Attributed record with date, scope and limits; testimony is not automatically fact. |
| Inspect an accessible reference | 10 minutes | Records visible damage, orientation and uncertainty; cannot prove unseen stability. |
| Take a local sounding/observation | 10 minutes | One dated observation at a reachable point. |
| Compare records or interpret a set | 10 minutes | Identifies whether the evidence supports the specific question. |
| Inspect an accessible route segment | 20 minutes | Records that segment, not an unseen entire crossing. |
| Set a temporary warning and record its location | 5 minutes | Communicates a known limit; does not repair terrain. |

Travel, difficult access, equipment repairs and repeat visits cost additional time. They are not included in those defaults. Parallel work is possible only when people can actually work independently. Do not silently double-count the same task.

### Evidence gate

A forecast needs an identifiable location, a usable reference, a current observation, relevant comparison and a declared horizon. A trend requires comparable observations, not one reading. A single accessible measurement may answer a narrower present-condition question. If evidence is insufficient, name the missing element and permit investigation or a narrower question; do not offer a roll that invents it.

### Interpretation check

Roll d20 +3 for relevant survey/fieldcraft training, otherwise d20. One useful assistant adds +2; assistants do not stack. Appropriate tools enable work but grant no automatic extra bonus. Clear facts require no roll.

DC10: minor ambiguity in an intact local record. DC13: reconcile conflicting observations with adequate references. DC16: interpret disturbed but still meaningful evidence. No roll answers a physically unobserved essential fact. Natural20 does not grant omniscience; natural1 does not cause terrain failure.

Below DC: provisional/inconclusive, identify a useful next check where possible. Meet DC: supported conclusion with explicit limits. Beat DC by5+: also identify the most useful missing observation or a discriminating warning sign. Failure does not automatically create a confident false answer. No reroll on unchanged evidence merely by asking again; a new observation, relevant witness, corrected reference or materially different approach can justify another attempt.

### Issue the card

Complete four lines: Observed / Expected / Not established / Withdraw if. Planning horizon defaults to the declared sortie, with a review no later than60 game minutes after the latest observation. This is a proposed review interval, not a promise that danger waits60 minutes or a real-world standard. The director may require a shorter interval based on known conditions, stated before departure.

Example: 'Observed: two comparable readings rising beside orchard wall. Expected: lowest foot segment may become unavailable during a long recovery. Not established: lower outlet or loaded-cart passage. Withdraw if: reference shifts, opening changes, or a newer reading contradicts this. Review in30 minutes.'

### Turn knowledge into preparation

A supported forecast allows one preparation benefit for its stated route/task, if the party spends10 additional minutes and has the required ordinary gear/access:

- Marked bypass: identify and inspect an actual alternative around one observed hazard. Avoid that hazard's check only if the bypass really exists and fits the party/load. No invented shortcut.
- Secured approach: use an appropriate existing anchor and serviceable line or similar suitable gear. Gain +2 on the first directly matching environmental save, or allow an adjacent helper +2, not both. This preparation bonus does not stack with another assistance/preparation bonus on that save and grants no universal climbing or rescue guarantee.
- Recovery plan: place accessible spare gear or supplies on verified waiting ground and name a withdrawal route. Its benefit is actual available equipment and access, not a dice bonus or automatic rescue.

A failed or incomplete forecast does not prohibit ordinary preparations supported by obvious facts. The supported-card benefit formalizes efficient planning, not ownership of common sense. Never grant protection against unrelated monsters or unseen hazards. A spent or invalidated benefit cannot be refreshed without appropriate new fieldwork.

### Review and withdrawal

Review due means reassess, not immediate catastrophe. Withdraw when a known reference moves, relevant works change, new evidence contradicts the conclusion, or the party materially changes its plan beyond the card's scope. Merely passing time changes information freshness, not the water by fiat. Update through observation, delivered reports or implemented communication, never global telepathy.

The director establishes the underlying situation and any event schedule before interpretation. Do not choose a hidden shift afterward solely to defeat a successful roll. A fair forecast can still have explicit uncertainty. If it fails, examine evidence, interpretation, delivery, changed conditions and decisions separately.

### Optional player interface specification

Field Journal -> New Forecast -> select route/task -> attach evidence -> see missing requirements -> choose field action with cost/exposure -> interpretation -> fill card -> choose feasible preparation -> pin card with review time. Display unknowns beside expected change. No green 'safe' badge. This is a UI proposal, not delivered code.

## 2. Rival faction: The Cutwardens

A small coalition of drainage workers and bank-maintaining households. They hold selected works they can maintain and defend, not the whole basin. Their reach and membership count remain unset. Wrapped fittings, broad shovels, hooked clearing poles, hauling straps and replacement stakes distinguish them; no grand uniforms.

Principle: 'If everyone needs it open, everyone owes a hand.' Contradiction: necessary maintenance becomes a claim to ownership. Missed work obligations can mean a household's approach repaired last or cargo held on waiting ground during a contribution dispute. Such conduct is witnessed and contestable, not an invisible tax on every traveler.

Against the Readers, they argue that dated knowledge is insufficient without someone repairing what it describes. The Readers distrust interventions whose downstream effects were not checked; Cutwardens distrust warnings issued by people who can leave. Both need each other. Neither is automatically a secret saboteur.

### Members

Hadrin Mott, work caller: broad, weathered, short peat coat with pale strap-worn shoulder patch and folded work list. Will stop visibly failing work, but too easily treats inability to contribute as refusal. Alarm makes him count unfinished jobs before remembering who cannot do them.

Sella Vey, opening keeper: lean, cropped hair, dark hood, mostly obsolete keys, wedges and retaining pins. Records previous opening positions. Cooperates with Neris and can close a route against Hadrin's wishes. She checks the mechanism before answering a demand to hurry.

Pellan Rusk, household advocate: former carrier, built-up right boot and slow deliberate gait. Notices when detours assume unburdened able-bodied travelers. Wants basic access guaranteed but lacks the labor to promise it honestly. His name establishes no relationship with Tavin Rusk.

First dispute: clear an opening before an expected rise, or wait for a downstream observation. Help obtain evidence, arrange a limited supervised opening or provide temporary storage elsewhere. No solution is required to reveal a villain. Berra Tull and Orenna Sedge are not automatically enrolled in this faction.

## 3. Artifact: The Rootstone Witness

An archaeological survey artifact, not automatically magical. Bronze-faced plate approximately18x12cm, attached by three surviving pins to a masonry footing in the Drowned Orchard. Green corrosion surrounds a polished horizontal reference groove. A bent corner suggests attempted removal. Beside it are a missing-bracket socket, older chisel marks, a diagonal crack and generations of initials.

Original: 'SET FROM THE STONE, NOT THE POST.' Later: 'POST MOVED. STONE NOT PROVED.' Faint addition: 'WITNESSED BY THREE. PAID BY TWO.' The last suggests a dispute without settling it.

A low-water interval exposes planting rows. An old sheet mentions a rootstone beyond a split pear, but the tree is gone. A sketch, socket alignment, witness or distinctive bent corner can locate the plate without pixel hunting.

States: discovered unlocks relevant questions; documented preserves location/orientation/inscriptions/damage; verified supports local comparison when independent evidence justifies it. Verification is revisable, not permanent certification.

To verify: inspect accessible damage, compare an independent reference, identify which old sheets refer to this groove, and determine whether their measurement method can be related to the current one. Missing evidence blocks verification rather than being replaced by a high roll. An interpretation check may resolve ambiguous surviving evidence under Read the Ground.

Dain can propose a replacement measuring bracket after documenting its socket. A new working method must distinguish new records from old measurements unless compatibility is established. It need not be a perfect reconstruction.

Benefit: verified station satisfies the reference requirement for appropriate orchard forecasts and may support comparison with identified older records. No global map reveal, guaranteed crossing, predator detection, free +3 bonus or proof of quarry causation. A new crack or incompatible reading can suspend it.

Claims: Readers want documentation in place; Cutwardens want accessible working copies; a descendant of an orchard household disputes unrestricted access across the old plot. Negotiate witnessed copies/access. Removing an undocumented plate destroys positional value even if the bronze remains. Possession and verified-station state must be recorded separately.

Inventory text: 'A hand-sized bronze plate, its groove worn bright beneath green corrosion. Several people corrected the inscription. None erased the person before them.'

## 4. Drowned Orchard events: d20

Roll once on a first meaningful entry, after a signaled change or during prolonged work. Do not reroll until combat appears. Entries are situations, not automatic damage. If incompatible with current state, adapt or reroll once. Record one-off discoveries and completed work; revisits do not reset them.

| d20 | Event and usable choice |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | A bent fruit-tree tag surfaces beside an ordered stump row. Record its location or ask who recognizes its mark; it is not automatically valuable metal. |
| 2 | A local claimant asks why the party is crossing the former plot. Show the survey purpose, negotiate a narrow access agreement, or withdraw; no automatic attack. |
| 3 | The Witness's suspected footing remains submerged. Inspect from accessible ground or return after another observation; no hidden demand to dive. |
| 4 | Two old sketches identify different 'split pear' trees. Compare wall sockets and notes instead of choosing a quest arrow. |
| 5 | A Greymaw watches an abandoned food basket on a dry ridge. Retrieve it with care, leave it, or determine ownership; its interest is food. |
| 6 | Fresh debris catches against a surveyed wall. Take a new observation before extending the previous forecast. |
| 7 | A Cutwarden offers to carry a replacement bracket in exchange for help moving boards later. Agree on a bounded task now. |
| 8 | A displaced marker has intact lettering but no trustworthy position. Preserve it as evidence without treating it as a working reference. |
| 9 | A sounding pole lodges beside a root. Withdraw, use a different reachable point, or recover it with appropriate help; no instant drowning. |
| 10 | Someone has chalked over the Witness's later warning. Compare prior copies and ask who cleaned it; intent is not automatically sabotage. |
| 11 | A personal object lies beside an old planting socket. Record its findspot and take it to the Return Shelf rather than assuming loot. |
| 12 | A runner arrives with a correction for a different route. Confirm whether it affects this trip instead of automatically withdrawing every forecast. |
| 13 | A distant head-and-ridge shape could be timber or Reed-jaw. Observe from verified ground; do not force an ambush regardless of choice. |
| 14 | A household wants the survey finished before lending its carrying frame. Negotiate collateral or partial work, not surprise lifelong debt. |
| 15 | A reference's crack is wider than the copied sketch. Suspend its use and seek an independent check; the artifact itself need not be lost. |
| 16 | A salvage party already has a witnessed record of the groove. Exchange copies, compare disagreements or commission their testimony. |
| 17 | A dry waiting patch is occupied by someone resting with an injured companion. Share space, help relocate safely or use another route; no punishment for slow movement. |
| 18 | A low-water interval reveals a better view of the orchard rows, not a newly guaranteed crossing. Sketch now or keep the planned return time. |
| 19 | Tapping begins beyond the last verified stump. Note direction and limits; debris may explain it, but the event confirms no supernatural origin. |
| 20 | A witnessed independent reading agrees with the documented Rootstone reference. Apply it as real comparison evidence, not automatic verification if other essentials remain missing. |

## 6. Short quest: Valid When Issued

Carrier Esken Vale returns without his handcart. His notice says foot passage was observed. 'I went on foot. I pulled the cart. Tell me where your difference begins.' Nobody died. Goods remain stranded; recovery matters. His surname establishes no relationship with Dren Vale.

Underlying sequence, established before investigation: the notice was honestly limited but easily misunderstood; Esken took a loaded handcart; Cutwardens altered a nearby opening after the observation; a correction reached Milebank but missed Esken after he changed his stated route. No fact alone absolves everyone.

Stage1: inspect an approach from verified ground, recover fragile goods or smaller loads, arrange equipment if needed. No mandatory fight. Predator signs complicate choices without explaining the forecast failure. A failed check need not erase all goods.

Stage2: compare original notice, work record, correction time and a witness to the changed route. Evidence reveals sequence, not a predetermined villain. Missing records can be replaced by qualified testimony where justified, not magically reconstructed.

Stage3: witnessed settlement. Readers may refund service and clarify wording; Cutwardens may supply recovery labor for late reporting; Esken may contribute a bounded task; a shared notice point can be installed before the route divides. Partial agreements remain valid. No forced perfect reconciliation.

Recovered goods remain recovered; notice improvements persist; revised terms appear in later contracts. Do not reset repairs or remove essential companions off-screen.

Closing: Esken reads 'Loaded handcart' from the revised sheet. 'Could have written that before.' Ulla says, 'Yes.' She leaves the old notice on the table.
