# The Glasshouse Clearing

Structured consolidation of the proposed negotiated evacuation, not an assault or implemented quest. Related: [complications](glasshouse-clearing-complications.md), [interception](remnant-goods-interception.md), [dialogue](hesh-challenges-oren.md), [Grey Lash](grey-lash.md).

## Objective

All six Kin leave Halwen, Sere reaches an inspected shelter, bounded restitution occurs, and Remnant gains glasshouse inspection access. Kin do not acknowledge a lord; forgiveness is not promised. Grey Lash has defined work and destination.

## Old Charcoal Lodge

Proposed abandoned woodland lodge off a lower track outside immediate estate grounds, close enough for a daylight move with realistic effort; exact distance unset. Standing rear wall, dry bay, damaged outer room, reachable water whose reliability needs checking, frame-wide entrance, no occupants found. Viable temporary shelter, not permanent safety.

Kett and Vey first inspect escape paths and cart approach. Moth later inspects or receives enough honest information to consent; determines patch needs. Evidence of occupation requires new planning before glasshouse dismantling. Inspection proves observations, not eternal security.

## Terms at chapel court

Elsin records, reads, corrects practical terms. Hesh can require oral repetition rather than pretend fluency in unfamiliar legal formulas.

Oren: keep specified route open; prevent unauthorized seizures; bounded bundle inspection; occupy glasshouses only after sixth departs; pay agreed transport/clearance share. Hesh: six occupants leave, identify placed hazards along route, return listed goods, agreed carrying limit, no further theft during operation. Grey Lash: acceptable transport, assist all six to lodge, agreed load, remain until Sere settled and goods arrived, reassess unusable route. Destination obligation is repeated: not merely chapel court.

No agreement settles estate title, erases Garden worker’s injury, or creates future service.

## Separate exchanges

Remnant pays Grey Lash for operation; Kin contribute selected salvage/information/agreed payment for transport; injured worker or actual representative receives separately negotiated restitution. Oren cannot settle another person’s injury unilaterally. Boots cut apart require usable substitutes or bounded accepted compensation. Refusal leaves dispute explicit; apology is not footwear.

## Inspection and crossing

Owners open bundles in view. Estate-marked repaired fitting can be disputed without voiding whole agreement: allowance, substitute, deferred claim, or new evidence. Rauth may overreach; Oren must enforce limits. Sere herself consents before movement. Frame carried through prepared section with relief; others handle goods without sealing someone behind inspection. Vulnerability is having dismantled old shelter before reaching new, not mandatory violence.

Moth leaves last after checking bench, sorting bed, old drip; retrieve small forgotten objects unless genuine visible danger prevents it. Hesh confirms six present before Remnant enters range. Empty space still contains structural hazards and broken glass; occupation does not restore functionality.

## Journey and completion

Transfer frame to prepared cart at platform; maintain agreed load, rest Brik after carrying. A final cart-inaccessible approach may require a longer carry but is manageable, not proof inspection was pointless. Marda checks daylight, Vey prepares, Tern insists on rest.

Complete when all six reach lodge, Sere settled dry, belongings present, company gear recovered, payment/exceptions acknowledged. Moth organizes sleeping, Kett checks exits, Iss checks bundles, Sere demands tools. Ordinary irritation resumes. Marda promises no automatic return; any Vey commitment must be specific.

## Lasting consequences

Kin retain members/shelter but need food/repairs. Remnant gains space without battle but bears restoration and title dispute. Grey Lash earns pay with unresolved crew disagreements. Gardens sees reduced raids but strengthened Remnant position. Success is real, not universally beneficial.

## Closing scene beat

Moth’s old screen does not fit new opening. Sere demands support. Brik sits before being assigned more. Hesh gives final fitting to Marda. Asked if everyone is present, he sees six sleeping places being argued over and says yes. Marda takes payment and leaves.
