Orlen is about 250 miles from the Wrack Shore to the Long Marrow and 250 miles from the scarp to the sea. The walk from the highland edge to Wexhaven is about 315 miles and takes 28 days, the whole of the four-week thaw. The Slate Sea is about 1,400 miles across, and the Round is about 4,500 miles of coast. The owner accepted all of it on 2026-11-19 (O10); the few distances the lore already fixed are kept and marked.


The walk south runs down the Bell Road, east along the Causeway and down the Salt Trace, because nobody crosses the drowned middle of the fen in a straight line.

## How distance is counted

Nobody in Orlen counts in miles. They count in days: about eight hours of grey light, on foot, carrying a load, in winter. Carts go no faster, because the roads are bad and the beasts are weak.

| Ground | Miles in a day | Where |
|---|---|---|
| Kept road | 12 | The Old Causeway, the lower Bell Road, the Downs road |
| Climbing road | 8 | The upper Bell Road, the upper road, the Marrow Passes |
| The Salt Trace's crossings | 9 | The fen stretch of the Trace, with its fords and ferry |
| The dry ways, at low water | 6 | Under the drowned middle, by the stakes |
| Off the road | 8 | Upland, moor, forest, the Scald |
| Under the hills | 10 | The Hewers' Road: graded, dry, no weather |
| Carrying the dead | 11 | The Corpseway, with a stretcher and rests at the benches |
| A punt on the fen | 15 | Where there is open water |
| Under sail, fair weather | 100 | The Slate Sea |

**The bells.** A carrying bell is heard clearly at the next tower about **eight miles** off, and further in still air.

**The Salt Trace**, Brackenreach to Wexhaven: about 157 miles, fourteen days. The fen stretch is 36 miles and four days: Long Wade at 6, the Ferry Stump at 12, the White Trees half way at 18, Pole Bridge at 24, and the southern reach at 36. **The Corpseway**, the Watchhouse's parish end to Saint Orra's Steps: 23 miles west, two days with a stretcher. Its twenty-two pairs of benches stand a mile apart.

**The Marrow Passes**, Dunmarrow's spur to the Weald Steps: about 60 miles, seven or eight days, all of it ork ground.

## What the lore fixes, and what is new

**Fixed by the lore, and kept:** the ledge to the Slagcrown, a day and a half on foot, due west (O6, P11). The toll house half way along the Causeway. Saint Ebbe's two days east of the Watchhouse. The Weald eight days deep. A week under sail to the Sable Shore. The Round in a season. The Red Country a year by camel.

**New:** every mile figure, the pace table, every size and every stage above.

## The game's scale (accepted 2026-11-19)

1. **One cell is one metre,** as P33 already has it.
2. **The Chart is true to this tab.** A leg costs its days, and the days are these.
3. **Built places at true size.** The Slagcrown's crown is half a mile across, about 800 cells. The Watchhouse is 8 m.
4. **Open country at one tenth.** A day's walk of 12 miles (about 19 km) becomes about 1.9 km on the Ground, about twelve minutes of walking at the engine's 2.6 cells a second. At this scale the Blackfen is about 18 km by 9 km, and all of Orlen about 40 km square.
5. **Far landmarks keep their true bearing** and are drawn larger than true on the backdrop, as the key painting does. P11's sixty cells to the Slagcrown stays.

## Accepted by the owner, 2026-11-19

1. The pace table, and a day as eight hours on foot.
2. Orlen at about 250 miles each way.
3. The scarp at about 60 miles, in the order Hob's Height, the Slagcrown, Greystair, Dunmarrow, Kell Tor.
4. The Bell Road at about 96 miles, with its towers eight miles apart.
5. The Causeway at about 60 miles.
6. The walk south taking the whole thaw.
7. The game's two scales: built places true, open country at one tenth.
