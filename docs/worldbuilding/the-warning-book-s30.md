# The warning book: the winter of S.30

**Approved 2026-11-19 (O10 in `CANON.md`).** Written 2026-11-15 from the novel's beats (*Ashen Wilds: the novel
(the winter of S.30)*). The novel opens every chapter with one entry from the Watchhouse's warning
book. Collected in order, those thirty entries are the book's record of the winter that ends at the
thaw where *The Long Account* begins. This file sets them out as an item for the game.

Everything here follows `bestiary.md` (the warning book as the Watchhouse keeps it),
`items-and-texts.md` (proposal 11, the Warning-Book Page, and the text conventions of section 4) and
`godot-people.md` (Margery Reeve, its keeper since S.12).

## The item

| | |
| --- | --- |
| Name | **The Warning Book (winter of S.30)** |
| Kind | A readable object at a shelter fire, built from Warning-Book Pages (`items-and-texts.md`, proposal 11) |
| How it fills | Each page is found where its entry happened. Brought to the fire, it is "returned to the book" and its text joins the readable book in chapter order. Empty places stay blank, with the chapter's number |
| Weight | Pages 0.01 kg each. The book itself does not leave the fire |
| Hand | Margery Reeve's, signed *M. Reeve*. Copied boards and slates are marked as copies. The last entry is in Rafe Sedley's hand, with Margery's fair copy under it |
| Text | Plain ASCII, as the canon sheet requires for every panel and page |

**Why it is an item and not only a story.** The book is the Watchhouse's memory, and the rule of the
whole Blackfen is that what is known is kept apart from what is guessed. Every entry here says what
was seen, and most of them say what nobody knows. A player who reads it learns the world the way the
house did: one dated, signed line at a time.

**Where each page is found** (approved under O10). Places use the atlas and floor plans (see *Ashen Wilds atlas*
and *Ashen Wilds floor plans*).

## The entries

### 1. The Outside Fire

*Found: the Watchhouse, nailed to the fence post by the Outside Fire.*

```text
Greymaw on the south wet. Nine, or six counted twice.
They have been pacing something for three days and we do
not know what. If one comes to you low with its tail down,
it is not tame. Do not whistle.
M. Reeve.
```

### 2. In the Passage, Thrust

*Found: the Watchhouse, the Counting Passage.*

```text
Two goblins left in the house, we think. One in the yard by
the door, one at the hall end of the passage. They wait for
a turned back. We do not know what is under the floor.
M. Reeve.
```

### 3. The Drain

*Found: the Watchhouse hearth, wedged in the hearth stones.*

```text
The hearth drain runs under the passage to the yard ditch.
Whoever stops it stops it from above, with stone. There is
stone in the fallen lean-to.
M. Reeve.
```

### 4. Winna's Pot

*Found: the Long Stable. A copy of the notice nailed to the stable pier.*

```text
It will not come through a door. That is why ours is narrow.
```

### 5. Three Names

*Found: the Naming Stone.*

```text
Three names were scratched on the Naming Stone this autumn,
in three hands. None of the dead were limed. We do not know
who scratched them.
M. Reeve.
```

### 6. The Dry Ways

*Found: Low Harrow, the sluice-house.*

```text
The road is walled at the gap. No carts since autumn. The
grey rags went round it, east, and came back. We do not know
how.
M. Reeve.
```

### 7. The Gap

*Found: the Causeway tower. A copy of the tower roster.*

```text
If they come in numbers, the tower holds and the road does
not. Fall back to the Watchhouse. Bring the book.
```

### 8. The Tower

*Found: the Causeway toll house.*

```text
Travellers say the tower stands roofless with the Watch's
dead still inside. The door is stopped with stone, the sign
of a den. The bell lies in the yard with its tongue gone.
Nobody knows whether a beast did that or a person.
M. Reeve.
```

### 9. The Road Opens

*Found: the Old Causeway, the first passing place.*

```text
Carts on the Causeway, the first since autumn. A Coldbarrow
man selling stones that hold heat till morning. Greymaw
following the carts.
M. Reeve.
```

### 10. Lime

*Found: the Split Kiln.*

```text
The dead on the Causeway are fruiting. The Split Kiln has
lime and wants stone or fuel. The chalk cut is a troll's.
It cannot see. It hears everything.
M. Reeve.
```

### 11. The Last Gardens

*Found: the Last Gardens, the seed room.*

```text
The Gardens have stopped selling cloth. The cistern is
falling. Without their cloth we have no bandages.
M. Reeve.
```

### 12. The Culvert

*Found: Halwen House, the cistern vault.*

```text
The water at Halwen has three faults. Mending one mends
none.
M. Reeve.
```

### 13. The Kin

*Found: Halwen House, the glasshouse range.*

```text
Six road waifs hold Halwen's glasshouse range. The Remnant
wants it. A salvage worker was cut in their trench and lost
his boots.
M. Reeve.
```

### 14. The Approved Load

*Found: Halwen House, by the service door.*

```text
The move goes in the first gap between weathers. Every
bundle is listed. What is not listed stays.
M. Reeve.
```

### 15. The Third Arch

*Found: the Roofed Mile, south mouth. A copy of Annet's board.*

```text
ROOFED MILE. Occupied. DO NOT ENTER FOR SHELTER.
A man died beyond the fifth pier. We do not know his name.
```

### 16. The Fold

*Found: Saint Vey's Fold, the front dormitory.*

```text
Saint Vey's Fold. Orks in the stone rear range, travellers
in the timber front. Nobody crosses the court at night.
A priest from the south has come with papers.
M. Reeve.
```

### 17. The Bargain

*Found: the Watchhouse hearth.*

```text
Lending fire tonight. Every household comes for a coal.
M. Reeve.
```

### 18. The Drowned Lantern

*Found: the Drowned Lantern, the taproom.*

```text
The band winters at the Drowned Lantern, east. Corwen Dole
keeps a tally of every share.
M. Reeve.
```

### 19. The Letters

*Found: Brackenreach, the Upper Row.*

```text
Three letters are going south over our Causeway. What they
say decides what comes up the road in spring.
M. Reeve.
```

### 20. The Crossing

*Found: the Old Causeway, a passing place.*

```text
Dead Calm. Nobody moves.
M. Reeve.
```

### 21. Pim

*Found: the White Trees, at the foot of the lookout steps.*

```text
A man at the White Trees with a Watch cloak. He has cut
steps in a trunk to watch both roads.
M. Reeve.
```

### 22. The North Road Goes Quiet

*Found: the Watchhouse door.*

```text
No heat-stones for two weeks. Old Edrin's runner, with one
sentence: "The north road is going quiet."
M. Reeve.
```

### 23. Take Nothing You Cannot Carry

*Found: Coldbarrow, the north gate.*

```text
No heat-stones. Coldbarrow is empty or nearly. The Bent Span
is settling. Whatever goes north goes on backs from
Coldbarrow.
M. Reeve.
```

### 24. The Hammers

*Found: Greystair, the bloomery.*

```text
Greystair. The Bloomery Company makes heat-stones from the
spoil. The listeners have asked them to stop. Something is
coming up out of the hollow at night.
M. Reeve.
```

### 25. The Venting Slopes

*Found: the venting slopes, on a cord of miners' tags.*

```text
Between Greystair and the Slagcrown the ground is warm and
the snow does not lie. The miners' dead are there with their
tags. Torches burn fast.
M. Reeve.
```

### 26. The Quiet

*Found: the Low Galleries. A copy from a listener's slate.*

```text
[A drawing of a hand, closed.]
I do not know what this says. I have copied it as it was.
M. Reeve.
```

### 27. The Slagcrown

*Found: the Slagcrown, the hall.*

```text
The Slagcrown. Nobody has come back from it to tell this
book anything.
M. Reeve.
```

### 28. The Weight Gate

*Found: the Black Seam drift-head, on the scaffold. A copy from Aud's slate.*

```text
Against the heat. Against the Tenant. Against what comes up
the drift. Three hands wrote why the gate was built. None of
them wrote why it was left.
M. Reeve, copied from Aud's slate.
```

### 29. The Mouth

*Found: the Black Seam drift, above the Weight Gate.*

```text
Dead Calm. Nobody moves.
M. Reeve.
```

The same words as entry 20. In the novel Rafe moves anyway.

### 30. The Thaw

*Found: the Watchhouse fire, in the book itself. This page is never found loose.*

```text
Sealed. We do not know against what.
R. Sedley.

Sealed. We do not know against what.
M. Reeve, fair copy.
```

## For the game

- **The readable book.** At the fire, the book opens to the earliest returned page. Missing pages
  show their number and nothing else. With all thirty, the last page shows both hands.
- **No spoilers by position.** Pages from Part Three (23 to 30) are found only past Coldbarrow, so
  a player cannot read the ending at the Watchhouse.
- **Entry 30 is the exception.** It appears in the book by itself once the gate scene is done, and is
  never found loose.
- **The book and the bestiary.** `bestiary.md` is the warning book's beast pages. These thirty are its
  daybook for one winter. They sit side by side at the fire.
