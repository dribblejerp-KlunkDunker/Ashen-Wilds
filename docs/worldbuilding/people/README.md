# Who is who

Every named person in the Ashen Wilds lore, the atlas and the novel, with where to find their full entry. States are
as of the winter of S.30 (the novel) or the thaw of S.30 that follows it (The Long Account). Compiled 2026-11-19, when the owner accepted
all proposals and asked for every character to have a full entry (ruling O10 in `../CANON.md`).

New full entries are in this folder, by where people are found:

- [`the-watchhouse.md`](the-watchhouse.md): the refuge, the Causeway Watch, and the names on the Watchhouse's texts
- [`the-fen.md`](the-fen.md): Stakeby, Brackenreach, Moss Hythe, Saint Ebbe's, the meres, Merewick, the Steps, Dunmere, the Burning Bank and the lost towns
- [`the-bell-road-and-the-measures.md`](the-bell-road-and-the-measures.md): Halwen House, the road, Rook's Stair and the Drowned Measures
- [`the-citadels-and-the-kingdom.md`](the-citadels-and-the-kingdom.md): the Breaking, the Slag-Lords, the highland edge and the kings
- [`the-round.md`](the-round.md): Wexhaven and the voyage
- [`the-novel.md`](the-novel.md): Rafe Sedley and Merrick Thwaite

Older full entries stay where they were written, and the table points to them.

**Settled (O11):** the Merewick Mara Venn and Harl are the only ones; the lost faction bible's road carrier and
toll-keeper are retired with it.
