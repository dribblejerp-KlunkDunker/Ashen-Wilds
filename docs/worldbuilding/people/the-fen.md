# People: the Blackfen beyond the Watchhouse

Everyone named in the fen's towns, roads and hollows, and the people the eight background survivors lost. Ages
and states are as of the winter of S.30. Each entry ends with its sources and what was new on 2026-11-19
(ruling O10). Full entries that already exist elsewhere: the Dunmere households (Renn, Senn, Vey) in
`dunmere-first-arrivals.md`; the band and the future companions (Dace Ormer, Avice Stoke and the rest) in
`godot-people.md`.

## Stakeby and the stake-hands

### The stake-hands of Stakeby
*A hamlet's people · scattered since the autumn of S.29*

**Who.** Four generations of families paid by the Crown, and after S.3 by the Watch's salt, to drive and keep the oak
stakes that hold the Causeway's footings and mark the dry ways. They know *the word* that tells a stake-road from a
drowned one. When the tower fell the salt stopped and Stakeby broke up. Edda Wray is one of them, and so are the
two who walked to the Watchhouse in the novel ("We set out four. They let two of us go.").
**Looks.** Thick shoulders from the beetle, the stake-driver's maul; shins scarred by the stakes' iron caps.
**Carries.** A beetle, a coil of marking cord, a bundle of seasoned oak kept wet so it will not split.
**Find them.** In the empty houses at Stakeby, on the dry ways, at the Watchhouse door asking for nothing.
**Hooks.** Only a stake-hand can re-drive the Nine Stakes' rotten boundary stakes. Bring one back to Stakeby and the
dry ways can be kept again.

*Sources: `godot-people.md`; the atlas (Low Harrow and the dry ways); the novel, chapter 4. New: carries, the
re-driving hook.*

## Brackenreach

### Factor Lisle Gant
*Cinder Factor of Brackenreach · alive*

**Who.** The Factor house the Cinder Factor background carried for, nineteen years. Gant's waybill marks both loads
delivered on the day Ned Coppin drowned, and Gant paid for both.
**Looks.** A heavy man in a carter's coat that has never carried anything, with a counting-board on a strap.
**Wants.** Loads that arrive. **Fears.** A road nobody will walk at any price.
**Carries.** Waybills by the hundred; a strongbox of salt; the Factors' seal.
**Find him.** In the Upper Row, behind a shop barricade, above the head of the Salt Trace.
**Hooks.** He will hire anyone with a back for the Salt Trace, *paid on arrival, never before*.

*Sources: `godot-people.md` §2. New: everything but the name and the waybill.*

### Ned Coppin and Hanna Teague
*A carrier, drowned on the Salt Trace in S.27; his widow, alive in the Upper Row*

**Who.** Ned was the Cinder Factor's carrying partner and went into a broken crossing on the Salt Trace under a full
load. His partner delivered his load with their own, was paid for both, and never told his widow the second
payment was his. **Hanna Teague** lives in Brackenreach's Upper Row.
**Looks.** Hanna: grey before forty, a ladder-key on her belt, Ned's carrying strap over her shoulder for a scarf.
**Wants.** Hanna may want neither the salt nor the truth.
**Carries.** Hanna: the key to her ladder; Ned's spare boots, which she will not sell.
**Hooks.** The second payment: forty measures of salt walked to Brackenreach with the truth.

*Sources: `godot-people.md` §2. New: Hanna's looks and carries.*

### Piers Ferrant
*Seller of ladder passage · Brackenreach Upper Row · alive*

**Who.** He sells ladder passage and salvage rights in the Upper Row. Paying him for the Lower Market's wheelwright
timber does not stop anyone else from paying too.
**Looks.** Clean hands, a fox-fur collar, a different ladder-key for every house on the Row.
**Wants.** To sell the same thing twice. **Fears.** The night the Row's ladders stay down.
**Voice.** Friendly, always, and never answers the question asked.
**Carries.** Ladder-keys; a ledger of who has paid for what (more than one name per line); a small purse of Port Sable
silver nobody else in the fen has.
**Find him.** At the top of the Upper Row's ladders at dusk, when he pulls them up.
**Hooks.** II.k The Lower Market. Ask where the silver came from: a Collector came up the Trace last autumn.

*Sources: `acts-two-and-three.md` (II.6, II.k); the atlas. New: looks, voice, carries, the silver.*

## Moss Hythe

### Amis Brough, Brede and the cutters' gang
*Peat-cutters · Amis dead in S.24; Brede alive and alone*

**Who.** The Peat-cutter background cut the Moss Hythe banks north of the Watchhouse with their sister Brede and a gang
of six. **Amis Brough**, the gang's eldest, went through a skin of moss into a sink in S.24; three of the gang died of
the cough. **Brede** lives alone in the gang's turf hut beside the stack cut in the last thaw, enough dry peat for the
hearth all winter. She will not give it to a refuge that argues every winter about letting coughers in, because
she coughs too.
**Looks.** Brede: a cutter's arms, a rope belt with a slane hooked through it, a turf-ash cough she covers with her
whole forearm.
**Wants.** Brede wants to be asked in, not let in. **Fears.** Being the cougher they argue about.
**Carries.** Brede: the stack (a winter's peat), a slane, the gang's cutting tally. Amis's slane is still in the sink.
**Find her.** In the turf hut on the high fen, about seven miles north of the Watchhouse.
**Hooks.** The last stack: carry the peat, or bring Brede in, or leave both.

*Sources: `godot-people.md` §2; the atlas (Moss Hythe). New: Brede's looks and wants.*

## Saint Ebbe's at Wick Hollow

### Father Anselm Crewe
*Priest of Saint Ebbe's · left one night in S.22 · alive or dead, unknown*

**Who.** He stayed when his superiors left, and then left too, one night in S.22, with the silver lamp. The chapel
cleaner kept Saint Ebbe's open alone for seven more years.
**Looks.** Remembered as a stooped, gentle man who sang flat.
**Carries (if found).** The silver lamp.
**Hooks.** He may be alive, which the cleaner says is worse. The Keeping in Exile at Saint-Aumere has a Brother Anselm
on its rolls of the returned. Nobody has checked.

*Sources: `godot-people.md` §2; the atlas. New: looks, the Saint-Aumere roll.*

### Wilkin
*The cleaner's son · died of the flux in S.19*

**Who.** Buried in Saint Ebbe's chapel yard.
**Carries (what is left).** A wooden horse on his grave, replaced every year until the autumn of S.29.
**Hooks.** The cleaner wants the horse replaced once more.

*Sources: `godot-people.md` §2; the atlas. New: the horse.*

## The meres and the reed hamlets

### Hawise Scarth
*Fen healer of the meres north of Merewick · dead about S.15*

**Who.** She taught the Fen Healer background, who walked her round for fifteen years after she died. She kept a
book of her own, and in it wrote the name of one of the two people the warning book says came back from silence:
Sib Arden.
**Carries (what is left).** Her book, in her hut on the meres; her bone needles; a jar of honey gone to sugar.
**Hooks.** Her book is the only other record of the two who came back.

*Sources: `godot-people.md` §2. New: what is left in her hut.*

### Mab
*The Fen Healer's daughter · born small in S.15, lived four days*

**Who.** Named and buried; nothing else is written.
**Hooks.** None. She is one of the losses the healer carries.

*Sources: `godot-people.md` §2.*

### Sib Arden
*Of the reed hamlets beyond Merewick · says she came back from silence · alive, if she was ever sick*

**Who.** The warning book records two people who say they came back from ash-lung's third stage. Hawise believed this
one. The answer stays uncertain: a misjudged sickness, a starving woman who was never sick, a true recovery nobody
can repeat, or a story told to get back inside a door.
**Looks.** A woman of about forty who speaks very softly, as if she is still not sure her voice is hers.
**Carries.** Hawise's note about her, folded small.
**Find her.** In the reed hamlets beyond Merewick.
**Hooks.** The healer must decide what to write.

*Sources: `godot-people.md` §2. New: looks.*

## Merewick

### Mara Venn of Merewick
*Copyist · Merewick · alive*

**Who.** The chapel cleaner for nineteen years, who, when the priest fled, took the register out from under his desk.
The village cut it apart with sewing shears so each family could keep its own rights. Later she became the village's
copyist, and was accused of appointing herself priest. When the collector came she produced the miller's duty
fragment and insisted that Harl's widow's cart claim be deferred, not discharged.
**Looks.** Ink in the creases of her palms, a cleaner's apron she still wears, spectacles bought with salt.
**Wants.** Every line read in context. **Fears.** Being made the thing they accused her of.
**Carries.** Copying paper bought with salt; the register's surviving gatherings; a bone folder.
**Find her.** In the chapel's record room.
**Hooks.** The Revised Register (II.l): she can read the hand and date of the Causeway leaf.

*Sources: `keeping-and-merewick.md`; the atlas (Merewick). New: looks, carries. This is the Merewick Mara Venn; the
lost faction bible also names a Mara Venn who was a Cinder Factor road carrier. Whether they are one person, two, or
one renamed is still open (CANON Q2).*

### Eda
*The orphan who keeps her reed grant in two copies · Merewick · alive*

**Who.** Her dead household's assessment was burned and its reed-cutting right saved for her. Widow Senn took her in,
kept the grant in curtain cloth in a crock, and taught her the boundary from a missing post to a bent willow. Senn
died in the second winter. Eda recovered the curling original and got Mara's copy, and keeps both. She dries the
pages when Mara copies. She is not Edda Wray.
**Looks.** Grown now; reed-cuts on her hands; the crock on a shelf above her bed.
**Carries.** The grant, twice. A reed-hook.
**Hooks.** A collector has begun considering the grant's yield.

*Sources: `keeping-and-merewick.md`; CANON (Eda is not Edda). New: looks, carries.*

### Widow Senn
*Of Merewick · died in the second winter*

**Who.** She proposed cutting the register rather than burning it, and took Eda in.
**Carries (what is left).** The crock, now Eda's.

*Sources: `keeping-and-merewick.md`.*

### The miller and his son
*Merewick's mill · the miller elderly or dead; his son alive*

**Who.** The miller held the diversion grant, with its duties: keep the bridge, let the water pass downstream. He
presented the right without the duty; Mara produced the duty fragment; the collector recognised the grant with its
obligations and marked him with a hooked stroke that means *interested witness*. Rumour made it *liar*. In a later
vignette, his son, grown old, takes a new boy to the chapel to read the mark in its context.
**Carries.** The miller's measures, levelled on an open table so anyone can watch.
**Hooks.** The patched bridge, kept by the miller's duty, shifts every winter.

*Sources: `keeping-and-merewick.md`; the atlas.*

### Harl of Merewick, his widow and their daughter
*Harl dead; his widow and daughter alive*

**Who.** Soldiers took Harl's cart north with his son; neither returned and no discharge was recorded. He demanded his
debts be read when the register was cut. His daughter's naming strip was lost overnight; the child knew her own
name without the proof. His widow's cart claim was deferred, not discharged, and the deferral was written.
**Hooks.** A living witness to the requisition would settle her claim. The Grain March took carts north in S.2.

*Sources: `keeping-and-merewick.md`; the atlas. This Harl is distinct from the faction bible's elderly Harl the
Toll-Keeper, or unresolved (CANON Q2).*

### Collector Brannick
*A collector of the Tithe · named only*

**Who.** Named in `keeping-and-merewick.md` only to say that the unnamed collector who came to Merewick must not be
identified with him without an explicit decision.

*Sources: `keeping-and-merewick.md`.*

## Saint Orra's Steps

### Mother Hilde Brennock
*Leader of the Keepers of the Steps · alive*

**Who.** One of five old women and men who stayed when the priests went south and elected themselves. She has named
more dead than anyone alive, and renews the threshold at the Steps.
**Looks.** Very old, very straight, a rope girdle with a knot for every hundred names.
**Wants.** The threshold renewed by someone who will outlive her. **Fears.** That nobody will.
**Carries.** The Steps' oil and the hospice keys; a staff cut from the rock's one thorn tree.
**Find her.** In the hospice on the rock's shoulder, or on the stair at dusk lighting the lamp.
**Hooks.** Whether the threshold holds if nobody renews it.

*Sources: the atlas (Saint Orra's Steps). New beyond the atlas: looks, wants, carries.*

### Brother Bede
*Keeper of the Register Hall · alive · blind*

**Who.** He keeps the Register Hall by touch, reading the cut names of the dead with his fingertips.
**Looks.** Milk-white eyes, fingertips worn smooth, a habit dusted with stone.
**Carries.** A chisel and mallet for new names; a list he cannot read and keeps anyway.
**Find him.** In the Register Hall inside the rock.
**Hooks.** Bring him a name and he will cut it. He knows every name on the west fen's walls, including some that
should not be there.

*Sources: the atlas. New: looks, carries, the hook.*

## Dunmere

### Reeve Aldous Penk
*Chair of the Wardmote · Dunmere · alive*

**Who.** He chairs the twelve householders who govern by the Conditions (residence, a useful trade, provisions
brought, current registration), which they have never repealed. He believes, with reason, that the Conditions saved
the town.
**Looks.** A heavy chain of office, a ledger under his arm, a bad hip he will not mention.
**Wants.** The town fed through one more winter. **Fears.** The Lee, and the people who used to camp in it.
**Carries.** The key to the inner gate. A copy of the Conditions in his own hand.
**Find him.** In the Moot Hall.
**Hooks.** The Granary Vault: he decides each winter who eats.

*Sources: the atlas (Dunmere). New: looks, wants, carries.*

### Ysolde Vey
*Gate-reeve of Dunmere · alive*

**Who.** She reads the Conditions at the gate and keeps Garran's Lantern lit. She is the Vey family's granddaughter
and has turned away more people than anyone alive.
**Looks.** A lantern on a pole, a cloak against the mere wind, her grandfather's name scratched on the lantern's frame.
**Carries.** Garran's Lantern; a copy of the Conditions; the gate tally.
**Find her.** At the gatehouse at the head of the Mere Causeway.
**Hooks.** What Garran Vey wanted to go back for (`dunmere-first-arrivals.md`).

*Sources: the atlas; `dunmere-first-arrivals.md`. New: looks, carries.*

## The Burning Bank

### Ada Wellock
*Leader of the Bank folk · the Warm Rows · alive*

**Who.** She leads about thirty people descended from the cutters who would not leave, who cook on the underburn and
never need fuel. She has lived underground for twenty years and can tell by the colour of a wall how long it has
been cooling. Most of the Bank folk cough.
**Looks.** Soot-black to the elbows, a cutter's leather apron, eyes that water in daylight.
**Wants.** The north side kept for the orks and the south for her people. **Fears.** The underburn going out.
**Carries.** Peat-bricks; a bog-oak carving knife; a fire-hook.
**Find her.** In the Warm Rows at the edge of the fire's reach.
**Hooks.** The Bell Pool: on still nights a bell sounds from under the water. She forbids anyone to take the Sleepers'
ornaments.

*Sources: the atlas (The Burning Bank). New: looks, wants, carries.*

## The lost towns

### Hulda, Ware and Agna Haskett
*Smiths of the Nailhouse · alive*

**Who.** **Hulda**, the eldest smith, will not sell a spear-head to anyone she has not fed first. **Ware**, her son, works
the treadle and keeps the count in the nail-loft. **Agna** goes out on the carts to the White Trees. Their forge turns
the fen's scrap into nails, rims and spear-heads; they swear slag-iron rusts slower than common iron.
**Carries.** Nails by the hundred, rims, spear-heads; slag-iron; Hulda's tongs, Ware's tally board.
**Find them.** The Nailhouse, nine miles south-south-west of the White Trees.

*Sources: the atlas (The Nailhouse); `items-and-texts.md` (the slag-iron claim). New: the family.*

### Garth Collier and Tam
*Drovers · Bellwether Yard in summer · alive*

**Who.** **Garth** has walked the Bell Road for forty years. **Tam** is his granddaughter. They bring the last flocks
down in summer and carry wether bells cast at Hob's Height, which bring a flock home through fog and leave the ringer
colder than the weather can explain.
**Carries.** A wether bell each; shears; a tally-stick; two dogs.
**Find them.** Bellwether Yard, twelve miles west-south-west of the Watchhouse, in summer only.

*Sources: the atlas (Bellwether Yard). New: all.*

### Dowsett Starke and Mattock
*Peat-cutters of the Nine Stakes · alive*

**Who.** **Dowsett**, Odo Starke's brother, keeps the bargain stone and the count of whose turn it is. He does not know
Odo is dead. **Mattock**, the oldest cutter, remembers the last re-driving of the stakes.
**Carries.** Cutting spades; the stone's tally; Mattock's knowledge of which family each stake bounds.
**Find them.** The Nine Stakes, ten miles east-north-east of Merewick.

*Sources: the atlas (The Nine Stakes). New: all.*
