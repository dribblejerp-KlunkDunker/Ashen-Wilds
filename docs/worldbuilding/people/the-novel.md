# People: *Held the Gate*, the novel of the winter of S.30

The novel, titled *Held the Gate* (O11; drafted in a Claude Doc, "Ashen Wilds: the novel (the winter of S.30)"), follows the Watch Deserter from the Watchhouse
door to the Weight Gate. Its proposals N1 to N20 were accepted on 2026-11-19 (ruling O10), so its two leads are canon.
Everyone else in its cast already has an entry: see `README.md` in this folder.

### Rafe Sedley
*The Watch Deserter · alive at the thaw · about 34*

**Who.** The Watch Deserter, named (N1). Joined at sixteen, about S.12, under Captain Darby and then Serjeant Agnes
Kemble; drilled on the targe because there was never enough mail. An old spear-thrust under the left arm from S.18
closed wrong. On the night the tower fell he went down the culvert, and thinks he heard the order, in a squall where
nothing can be heard (N2). He reaches the Watchhouse about nine weeks late, after Pim Haddow has left the muster book
there (N3). His issued sword carries a faded glim mark (N5). He closes the winter by writing *Merrick Thwaite, billman.
Held the gate.* on the line for the dead, and his own name on the line for those who ran (N20).
**Looks.** Lean, the Watch's grey cloak worn to the weave, a wooden targe on his back, the sword he was issued.
**Wants.** To be told he was ordered; to keep the road, since he can no longer keep the tower.
**Voice.** Little. Orders in a level voice. No when he is lying.
**Carries.** The arming sword with its faded glim mark; a wooden targe; a torch; two rags.
**Hooks.** He is not the deserter on The Long Account's ledge (O11): at the thaw he is at the Watchhouse, writing in the
muster book, and the ledge's deserter is an older Watch man sent north by Gytha Marrin. Watch issue swords carried glim
marks, so both have one.

*Sources: the novel's Bible and plan; `godot-people.md` §2 (Watch Deserter).*

### Merrick Thwaite
*Watch billman, the friend · stays at the mouth · his fate unseen · about 45*

**Who.** A Watch billman who taught Rafe the passage rule in his first week and his letters the winter after (N13). One of
the nine at the tower (N14). He followed Rafe down the stair and heard no order. His grey cough, hidden for two winters,
runs the lore's stages through the book: cough, crust, silence, and a moment that may be a return (N15). He is the one
who says *look at that*; those are his last spoken words, at the end of chapter 25. At the Weight Gate, silent and dying,
not knowing Rafe, he fights him on the scaffold; at the Mouth he comes back for a few breaths, signs the passage rule
and *go*, and drops the gate from below. One knock. Rafe never sees what happens to him.
**Looks.** Big, loud, a billhook on a pole, a scarf over the mouth from the second chapter on.
**Wants.** For Rafe to be all right. A fire he is allowed to sit at.
**Voice.** Loud, warm, joking at the worst moments. Calls women mistress. Lies only for Rafe.
**Carries.** A Watch bill; his letters-board, with the passage rule cut in it.

*Sources: the novel's Bible, plan and Part Three beats.*
