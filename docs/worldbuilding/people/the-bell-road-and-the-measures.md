# People: the Bell Road and the Drowned Measures

Everyone named on the Bell Road, from the Last Gardens to Coldbarrow, and in the Drowned Measures off its quarry
branch. Ages and states are as of the winter of S.30. Each entry ends with its sources and what was new on
2026-11-19 (ruling O10).

Full entries that already exist elsewhere are not repeated: Sella Morn, Bren Tal and Nera Halwen
(`the-last-gardens.md`); the Garden Hands, Dema Rusk, Vessa Dorn, Jerrit Salk and Noll Fen (`garden-hands.md`);
the Halwen Remnant, Serjeant Oren Vale, Rauth Kell, Lenna Marr, Elsin Thale and Perrin Holt
(`halwen-remnant.md`); the Gutter Kin, Hesh Split-Lip, Sere, Kett, Brik, Iss and Moth (`gutter-kin-members.md`);
the Grey Lash, Marda Cale, Tern Roke and Vey Sorn (`grey-lash.md`); Garran Wold
(`glasshouse-clearing-complications.md`); Alys Cope, Ivo Pargeter, Aldo Flitch and Gise Marrable (`godot-people.md` §4).

## Halwen House

### Lady Maudren Halwen
*The Halwen heir in the south · absent · alive*

**Who.** The southern lady whose orders Serjeant Oren Vale carries. She means to have the house, and through it the
road, and through the road the slag-iron: the Halwens' wealth was always a share of the Bell Road's traffic, and the
Bell Road's traffic was slag-iron. If the full report goes south, her surveyor is on the upper road by spring,
marking the Black Seam.
**Looks.** Nobody on the road has seen her. Her seal (a closed hand over a door) is on every order.
**Wants.** The road reopened under Halwen storage rights, with the Watchhouse registered as a Crown waystation.
**Carries (in the north).** Her orders, in Oren's chest; her seal on the Remnant's household roll.
**Hooks.** The letters going south (II.6) decide what she sends up the Bell Road in spring.

*Sources: `acts-two-and-three.md`; the atlas (Halwen House). New: the seal.*

### Lady Maudren's surveyor
*A southern surveyor · on the upper road in the novel's winter, if the report went south*

**Who.** He marks the drifts toward the Black Seam for spring and offers to pay the Bloomery Company for Greystair's
drifts. He is perhaps at the Slagcrown in the novel's winter.
**Looks.** A long coat with too many pockets, a brass sighting-staff, a cough he blames on the altitude.
**Carries.** A surveyor's chain, a sighting-staff, a book of bearings to the Black Seam, Port Sable silver.
**Hooks.** His bearings are the best map of the deep anyone has made since S.0.

*Sources: `acts-two-and-three.md`; the atlas (the Citadels). New: looks, carries.*

### Sabeth Rill
*Surveyor for the southern claimant · hidden on Halwen House's upper floor · alive*

**Who.** The Remnant is an advance household sent by the southern claimant who is paying her: she came first, to
reconnoitre the house, and now lives upstairs, caught between the people who commissioned her and the people whose home
she is helping them assess. Her notes describe which cottages of the Last Gardens are occupied and where their water
comes from. She asks one thing: *Do not let him search the upper rooms until I have taken my things.* Her honest report
counts the gardeners' improvements, and so proves both their claim and the estate's renewed value. She uses the chapel's
family passage in bad weather and has left a small offering behind the altar, perhaps the thread-wrapped nail.
**Looks.** Ink-stained cuffs, a surveyor's satchel, soft indoor shoes she never takes off upstairs.
**Wants.** To finish the commission without being the reason the Gardens are lost. **Fears.** Oren reaching her notes
first.
**Carries.** Her reconnaissance notes; a measuring cord; Lady Maudren's orders quoted from memory.
**Find her.** Halwen's upper floor, behind the shutter.
**Hooks.** II.d The Upstairs Shutter; II.6 The Report South. Whose hands her notes reach decides what the south is told.

*Sources: `acts-two-and-three.md`; `halwen-house-occupation-factions.md`; `halwen-house-private-chapel.md` §10. New: looks,
wants, fears, carries.*

### Saint Edras of the Kept Hand
*A local saint of the Keeping · long dead*

**Who.** The Halwens dedicated their chapel to him. Household tradition says he held open a burning storehouse door
until the people inside escaped. When the household left, a kitchen servant, Hessa Pell, took off her headcloth and
tied it over his face.

*Sources: `halwen-house-private-chapel.md`.*

### Hessa Pell
*Kitchen servant of Halwen House · gone south or dead · her headcloth is still on the saint*

**Who.** She covered Saint Edras's face with her headcloth when the household was abandoned: the first of the covered
faces in the Chapel of the Kept Hand.
**Carries (what is left).** The headcloth, still tied.
**Hooks.** The covered faces (`halwen-house-private-chapel.md` §3).

*Sources: `halwen-house-private-chapel.md`. No link to the Pells of the Drowned Measures.*

## The road

### Old Edrin
*Bell-tender · the Bell-Tender's House at the quarry junction · alive · about 70*

**Who.** He was never a priest. He keeps his bell's tongue wrapped in felt and sends a runner instead of ringing,
"because noise reaches unwanted listeners". The warning board Annet's party sent to the Watchhouse passed through his
hands. In the novel he is in Part Two.
**Looks.** A bell-founder's leather bib he has no right to, a white beard tucked into it, deaf in one ear from the
years he did ring.
**Wants.** The road quiet. **Fears.** The bell being rung by someone who does not know what it carries.
**Carries.** Bell-Tongue Felt; the bell frame's key; a runner's staff with a hollow head for messages.
**Find him.** In the warm room of the Bell-Tender's House, 46 miles up the road.
**Hooks.** Ask him to ring. He will explain, at length, why not, and then perhaps once.

*Sources: `acts-two-and-three.md`; `bestiary.md`; `items-and-texts.md`; the atlas; the Bible. New: looks, wants, carries.*

### Annet and her party
*Travellers on the Roofed Mile · fate unknown*

**Who.** Annet's party sent the warning board to the Watchhouse: *ROOFED MILE. Occupied.* Annet struck a pier, which
drew the animal to the pier, and she wanted one line in the warning book: *A man died beyond the fifth pier.* No link
to anyone else of the name.
**Hooks.** II.g: correct the board, and give the dead man his name.

*Sources: `acts-two-and-three.md`; `roofed-mile-third-arch.md`; `README.md`.*

### Edwy Pole and his widow
*Stone carrier, dead beyond the fifth pier of the Roofed Mile; his widow alive on Rook's Stair's middle terraces*

**Who.** He carried stone to the Split Kiln from Rook's Stair and died beyond the fifth pier: the man with the
copper-mended strap. Asking along the road finds his widow. "His name was Edwy Pole." That is the side quest's whole
reward.
**Carries (what is left).** The copper-mended carrying saddle, under the gallery.
**Hooks.** II.g The Copper Repair: bring the saddle or only the name.

*Sources: `acts-two-and-three.md`. New: nothing.*

### Fesk
*Eldest of the Roofed Mile waifs · went up into the roof · presumed dead*

**Who.** He taught the Gutter-kin background to walk soft, then went up into the gallery's roof space when the
sheep-drags began and did not come back, before the board read *ROOFED MILE. Occupied.* His scratched mark is the last
in the waifs' cache behind a loose stone at the fourth pier.
**Carries (what is left).** The cache: everything the waifs ever took and kept, and the marks that were their only
record.
**Hooks.** The gallery cache. The animal stays unidentified: signs, a cache, perhaps a mark, never an answer.

*Sources: `godot-people.md` §2 (Gutter-kin). New: nothing.*

### Farrier Ossa Denn
*Farrier at Darnel Cross · alive*

**Who.** Missing three fingers of her left hand, she works skilfully and refuses owners who hold the lead, because they
pull at the wrong moment. She dates the widening crack in the inn's split main beam with charcoal marks while seven
households sleep under it.
**Looks.** A leather apron burned through at the knee, a charcoal stub behind her ear.
**Carries.** Farrier's tools; horseshoe nails; charcoal; a sounding of the beam she will share for news.
**Find her.** In the farrier's shed at Darnel Cross, 26 miles up the road.
**Hooks.** II.f The Split Beam: dismantle or prop. She takes news as payment.

*Sources: `bell-road-locations.md`; `acts-two-and-three.md`; the atlas. New: looks, carries.*

### Brother Coll Hathe
*Recovery claimant for the southern Keeping · Saint Vey's Fold · alive*

**Who.** Appointed by the Keeping's southern hierarchy to "recover" the hospice, with two hired escorts and a certified
copy of the Fold's endowment. He wants the rear range cleared of the ork den.
**Looks.** Southern wool, clean boots, a travelling desk on one escort's back.
**Wants.** The Fold back under the hierarchy. **Fears.** Writing south that he failed.
**Carries.** The certified endowment; a letter for the south; Port Sable silver for the escorts.
**Find him.** In the Fold's front dormitory, 64 miles up the road.
**Hooks.** II.5 The Fold: clear the den, refuse, or wall the court. His letter is one of those that go south in II.6.

*Sources: `acts-two-and-three.md`; the atlas. New: looks, wants, carries.*

## Rook's Stair: the Benchwardens

### Brida Coyle
*Works the Hanging Shed · Rook's Stair · alive*

**Who.** The woman Rook's Stair called Ashbound, who works the Hanging Shed's rope lift for heavy loads down into the
fog. She can become a companion. She shares a surname with Hildy Coyle and there is no link between them.
**Looks.** Rope-burned palms, a lifting harness she never takes off, ash-grey hair tied with cord.
**Wants.** To be called by her name. **Fears.** The rope giving under someone else's load.
**Carries.** A rope lift's brake key; a coil of good hemp; her own rope-splicing fid.
**Find her.** In the Hanging Shed.
**Hooks.** II.j The Hanging Shed: tell, bring home, or leave.

*Sources: `acts-two-and-three.md`; the atlas. New: looks, wants, carries.*

### Mara Sorn
*Bench caller · Rook's Stair · alive*

**Who.** She assigns work and shelter, and will close a productive terrace when it becomes unsafe, but gives
unrecognised newcomers the worst inspected jobs. Not the Merewick Mara Venn.
**Carries.** The bench roll; the shelter keys.

*Sources: `rooks-stair-quarry-supplement.md`; the atlas.*

### Edda Vennick
*Sounding hand · Rook's Stair · alive*

**Who.** She checks routes and records new cracks, and will not mark uninspected stone safe for anyone, including Mara
when Mara needs its salvage. She knows the troll's observed habits. Not Edda Wray.
**Carries.** A sounding hammer; a crack-book with dated chalk sketches.

*Sources: `rooks-stair-quarry-supplement.md`; the atlas.*

### Tollen Rake
*Stores keeper · Rook's Stair · alive*

**Who.** He keeps tools and ration accounts, and has quietly extended debts because writing them off would show an
empty store.
**Carries.** The tool tally; ration chits that do not add up.

*Sources: `rooks-stair-quarry-supplement.md`; the atlas.*

### Sorrel
*Quarry worker · Rook's Stair · dead*

**Who.** Named on the quarry slate: the troll *came to where Sorrel's hammer fell and brought both fists down there.*
**Carries (what is left).** The hammer, under the scree where it fell.

*Sources: `items-and-texts.md`; `act-one-the-outskirts.md`.*

## The Drowned Measures

### The Silt Readers: Ulla Renn, Neris Kett and Dain Orrel
*Twelve readers at the Dry Table · alive*

**Who.** *A depth mark without a date is a lie. A reading is not a promise.* **Ulla Renn** keeps the sheets. **Neris
Kett** makes the field observations and records her failed soundings and uncertain references rather than cleaning
them out of a report; she gives immediate danger warnings freely. **Dain Orrel** repairs poles and escorts crossings.
Neris is not Kett of the Gutter Kin.
**Carries.** Level sheets; sounding poles; Neris's field journal with every wrong forecast kept beside the right ones.
**Find them.** The Dry Table.

*Sources: `silt-readers-and-drowned-measures.md`; the atlas.*

### Berra Tull and Orenna Sedge
*Bank keepers at Milebank and the Peg Houses · alive*

**Who.** **Berra**, in a shortened patched coat and brown kerchief, keeps the bank's inspection history. **Orenna**,
broad-handed in an oversized grey coat with shortened sleeves, wants the Readers' notation taught locally so residents
can keep their own records. Neither belongs to a faction.

*Sources: `silt-readers-and-drowned-measures.md`; `drowned-measures-playbook.md`.*

### The Cutwardens: Hadrin Mott and Sella Vey
*Drainage workers for hire · alive*

**Who.** **Hadrin Mott**, work caller: broad, weathered, a short peat coat with a pale strap-worn shoulder patch and a
folded work list. **Sella Vey**, opening keeper: lean, cropped hair, dark hood, mostly obsolete keys, wedges and
retaining pins; she will not certify a route she has not observed. At dusk Hadrin hires the party to escort Sella to an
orchard drainage opening.
**Carries.** Sella's keys and pins; Hadrin's work list.

*Sources: `drowned-measures-playbook.md`; `cutwarden-adventure-pack.md`.*

### Pellan Rusk
*Household advocate · the Measures · alive*

**Who.** A former carrier with a built-up right boot and a slow, deliberate gait. He notices when detours assume
unburdened, able-bodied travellers, and wants basic access guaranteed but lacks the labour to promise it. No link to
Dema Rusk.

*Sources: `drowned-measures-playbook.md`.*

### Esken Vale
*Carrier · the Measures · alive*

**Who.** A carrier who returns without his handcart in one of the regional events. No link to Oren Vale or Dren Vale.
**Hooks.** Where the handcart went.

*Sources: `drowned-measures-playbook.md`.*

### The Low Rack Four
*A salvage partnership below the narrowed channel · alive*

**Who.** **Mara Holt**, caller and tally keeper, with a waxed board case, who repairs every numeral that smears. **Osric
Dane**, hauler, in a patched shoulder harness, who checks each load aloud. **Fenna Wale**, sorter and cloth repairer,
hems tucked up, bone needles in a dry tin. **Tollen Pike**, runner and landing watcher, short coat, spare dry socks
wrapped in cloth.

*Sources: `cutwarden-adventure-pack.md`; the atlas.*

### The plot households
*The Reed Yard, the Three Steps and the Bent Fence · alive*

**Who.** **Hesta and Corren Marr**, adult siblings, at the Reed Yard: a reed-drying lean-to and a sleeping room on a low
stone sill. **Widow Elsweth Pell** and her adult son **Jory** at the Three Steps; Elsweth cannot manage the muddy
approach without help. **Devren and Alis Hobb**, with Alis's father **Noll**, at the Bent Fence, whose patched fence
could block the shared approach if it loosens, and who hold the neighbourhood's borrowed carrying frame.

*Sources: `cutwarden-adventure-pack.md`; `drowned-measures-playbook.md`.*

### Dren Vale and Maelin Voss
*Named only*

**Who.** Dren Vale is named only to say a surname establishes no link; Maelin Voss belongs to an earlier rumour table
that the Cutwarden pack does not use. Neither has a part yet.
