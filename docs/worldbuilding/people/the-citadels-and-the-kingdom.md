# People: the Citadels, the deep, and the kingdom's past

The Slag-Lords and the people of the Breaking, the listeners and the Bloomery Company of the novel's winter, and the
kings Orlen remembers. Each entry ends with its sources and what was new on 2026-11-19 (ruling O10). The three
shut doors stay shut: nobody here knows who made the Vaults, what the orks fled, or why the Unnamed rise.

---

## The Breaking (S.-2 to S.1)

### Slag-Lord Cerrin Vane
*Slag-Lord of the Slagcrown, House Vane · gone since S.0 or S.1 · fate not recorded*

**Who.** At midwinter before the Breaking he overruled the Hewers' Lodge on his own hazard, with the Keeping's treasury
and Abbot Hamon Strand behind him, and ordered the Black Seam followed past the fitted stone. The Deep-Seam Vaults
were broken open on his order in S.0. His hall is the Slagcrown's great hall, where an ork pack dens now.
**Looks (from the hall).** His portrait in the counting house shows a narrow man in black with a slag-iron chain of
office; someone has scratched out the eyes.
**Carries (what is left).** The chain of office is not in the hall. His orders are in the counting house's Seam Roll.
**Hooks.** The Seam Roll's last entries are his, and the word *did not answer* is in another hand.

*Sources: `CANON.md` O2; `the-kingdom-and-the-breaking.md`; `acts-two-and-three.md`; the atlas (the Citadels); the novel
(the Seam Roll). New: the portrait, the missing chain, the second hand in the Roll.*

### Ebba Cray
*Forewoman of the Slagcrown's deep crew · lost in the first survey, S.0*

**Who.** In S.-2 her crew, in the lowest level, the Eleventh Gallery, found the Black Seam. She stayed because her crew
stayed. Six went through the breach with her on the fourteenth day with lamps and line, and four came back. She was
not one of them.
**Carries (what is left).** Her line, still tied at the breach. Her tally-board, found in the Eleventh Gallery.
**Hooks.** Marget Sayle is one of the four; she has never described what she saw.

*Sources: `CANON.md`; `the-kingdom-and-the-breaking.md`. New: the line and the tally-board.*

### Ancel Brode
*Hewers' Lodge spokesman · dismissed in S.-1 · long dead*

**Who.** When the seam met fitted stone in the autumn of S.-1 the Lodge refused the ground, using its right of refusal,
and Brode spoke for it. He was dismissed. His granddaughter Edith speaks for the Low Lodge at Halsing.
**Hooks.** The right of refusal is his legacy, and Edith carries it.

*Sources: `the-kingdom-and-the-breaking.md`; `the-round-and-the-long-account.md`.*

### Abbot Hamon Strand
*Abbot of the Slagcrown's house of the Keeping · fate not recorded*

**Who.** He preached on stewardship and blessed the descent. His nephew, Prior Lambert Strand of the Keeping in Exile,
wants silence about that blessing.
**Carries (what is left).** The abbey's copy of the blessing, in the Slagcrown's chapel, if the orks have not burned it.

*Sources: `the-kingdom-and-the-breaking.md`; `the-round-and-the-long-account.md`. New: the abbey's copy.*

### Father Hamelin Lynde
*Under-priest of the Slagcrown · fate not recorded*

**Who.** He rang the alarm bell against orders until the steward's men pulled him off the rope, and half the Citadel
came up because of it.
**Hooks.** The bell he rang is still in the Slagcrown's chapel tower, its rope cut short.

*Sources: `the-kingdom-and-the-breaking.md`; the atlas. New: the cut rope.*

### Kit Ewell
*Slagcrown lamp-boy · lived to old age in the fen*

**Who.** A witness of the Breaking. He said the sound was low and long, like water, or wind in a shaft, or something
else; his old-age account is in the lore.
**Hooks.** Where in the fen he lived out his life, and whether he told anyone more.

*Sources: `the-kingdom-and-the-breaking.md`.*

### Aymer Blount
*Lord Treasurer · sailed with the fleet in S.3*

**Who.** He demanded the Slagcrown's arrears of the Crown's Fifth on a schedule in S.-6, and the pressure of that
schedule is part of why the seam was followed.
**Hooks.** If he reached Vauclere, his ledgers are at Saint-Aumere.

*Sources: `the-kingdom-and-the-breaking.md`.*

## The Slag-Lords and their houses

### Rohese Tarrant
*Slag-Lord of Greystair · last seen at its upper gate, S.2*

**Who.** Greystair made cast stove plates, was built down a hillside in terraces, took in refugees and died of it. She
was seen at the upper gate and not recorded after.
**Carries (what is left).** The Tarrant stove-pattern boards, in Greystair's pattern loft: the only way anyone could
cast a Greystair plate again.
**Hooks.** One Greystair plate buys a house in Port Sable.

*Sources: `the-kingdom-and-the-breaking.md`; the atlas. New: the pattern boards.*

### Bertram Corbet
*Slag-Lord of Dunmarrow · fate not recorded after S.2*

**Who.** Dunmarrow made blades and the Watch's swords and helmets. He held it through the winter of S.1 with the
garrison and obeyed the order of the Grain March, which took the garrison south with the north's grain in S.2.
Dunmarrow is ork-held now.
**Hooks.** The Marking Hall inside Dunmarrow, where marks were cut on slag-iron.

*Sources: `the-kingdom-and-the-breaking.md`; the atlas.*

### Adam Haddon
*Slag-Lord of Hob's Height · led its people out in S.1*

**Who.** Hob's Height cast the Bell Road's bells. After goblins in its shafts and orks on its crag he walked his people
out in the autumn of S.1.
**Hooks.** Where they went: some of the Bell Road's bell-tender families are Haddon folk.

*Sources: `the-kingdom-and-the-breaking.md`; the atlas.*

### Ralf Ottery
*Steward of Hob's Height · fate not recorded*

**Who.** In the summer of S.1, when goblins first came up the shafts, he had the bell-ropes cut so that panic would not
stop the ore carts.

*Sources: `the-kingdom-and-the-breaking.md`; the atlas.*

### Ysmay Marne
*Slag-Lord of Kell Tor · behind its mortared gate since S.1*

**Who.** Kell Tor kept the deep pumps and the assay house that stamped iron's grade. She wrote to the other Slag-Lords
after the Breaking, and then Kell Tor sealed itself from the inside. Its records say its lowest levels met walled
galleries already old when the Citadel was young. A shepherd saw smoke from the crag in S.17.
**Hooks.** Kell Tor is never entered. Her letter, if a copy survives in another Citadel, is the only word from inside.

*Sources: `the-kingdom-and-the-breaking.md`; the atlas (the Citadels, the Deep).*

### Wilmot
*Shepherd · saw smoke from Kell Tor in S.17*

**Who.** The only witness that something at Kell Tor was alive, or burning, sixteen years after it shut.

*Sources: `the-kingdom-and-the-breaking.md`.*

## The highland edge, S.30 to S.31

### Marget Sayle
*One of the four who came back · her bothy under the ledge · alive · 62*

**Who.** One of the four who came back from Ebba Cray's survey in S.0. She has never described what she saw. She lives
in a field-stone bothy under the ledge, in sight of the Slagcrown: one door with a bar, a banked peat fire. In The Long
Account, Gytha Marrin sends the company north for her, and she wants to say one word to the right people: *don't*. She
is sick.
**Looks.** A hewer's shoulders gone thin, a face weathered dark, hands that do not stop moving.
**Carries.** The bothy's bar; peat; a hewer's lamp she never lights.
**Find her.** The bothy (`la_bothy`), under the ledge.
**Hooks.** The witness: whether she goes south, and what she says at Halsing.

*Sources: `CANON.md`; `the-round-and-the-long-account.md`. New: looks, carries.*

### Aud
*A listener · leads the listeners, so far as anyone does · alive*

**Who.** She came down from the Low Galleries to beg the Bloomery Company to stop its hammers. Asked what the orks
fled, she signs *below*, and then *we do not know*: the most honest answer anyone in the game gives. She refuses to
burn. In the novel she teaches Rafe cold light in chapter 26. She is not the Causeway tower's serjeant.
**Looks.** Pale from the dark, wrapped tools, felt on her boots, speaks in the hand-cant.
**Wants.** The Quiet kept. **Fears.** Noise.
**Carries.** Cold light (a working, not an item); a wrapped hand-lamp; felt.
**Find her.** At fallen Greystair in the novel's winter, or in the Low Galleries.
**Hooks.** III.2 The Hammers at Greystair: silence, work under weather, or leave it.

*Sources: `acts-two-and-three.md`; the atlas (the Deep); the Bible; N5. New: looks, carries.*

### Wat Corley
*A listener · keeps the bells at Hob's Height · alive · old*

**Who.** An old man who lives alone on the Spiral at Hob's Height and keeps the bells wrapped, as his people swore to,
climbing to the belfry every day to check the Mother Bell's chain.
**Carries.** Felt and twine; the belfry key.
**Hooks.** If the Mother Bell is ever rung, he will know before anyone.

*Sources: the atlas (Hob's Height and the Bell-Spire).*

### Tobin Crane
*Leader of the Bloomery Company · fallen Greystair · alive*

**Who.** He was a boy in Greystair when it fell. He leads forty Coldbarrow and Dunmarrow folk who have relit
Greystair's bloomery, and answers the listeners' pleas with the Company's children. He refuses to seal the Weight Gate
unless the survivor feeds his people. In the novel his advance party under Dunstan Kerr dies at the Slagcrown, and
Rafe has to tell him.
**Looks.** Forge-burned forearms, a Greystair stove-plate bolted to his chest as armour.
**Carries.** The bloomery's tally; a hammer; the Company's iron.
**Find him.** Fallen Greystair.

*Sources: `acts-two-and-three.md`; the atlas; the novel's Part Three beats. New: looks, carries.*

### Nessa Lowde and Dunstan Kerr
*Of the Bloomery Company · both die in the novel*

**Who.** **Nessa Lowde** dies on the furnace night at Greystair (chapter 24). **Dunstan Kerr** leads Tobin's advance
party, found dead at the Slagcrown's drift-head (chapter 27).
**Carries (what is left).** Kerr's party's gear at the drift-head: lamps, line, a bloomery hammer.

*Sources: the novel's Part Three beats; N17.*

### Oswy
*Hewer on the Black Seam · lost in S.0*

**Who.** Gise Marrable's husband, in the Slagcrown's workings when the Vaults were broken. She wants to reach where Oswy
was, and does not expect to come up.

*Sources: `godot-people.md` §4.*

## The kings Orlen remembers

### Godric II, the Chartering King
*Died S.-181*

Granted the Deep Charter in S.-196: the deep to the five houses, for the Crown's Fifth. Remembered fondly in the
Citadels and not at all in the fen.

### Wynfrith, the Toll-Queen
*Reigned S.-151 to S.-119*

Founded the Causeway Watch in S.-148, raised the toll house and built the waystations. The fen cursed her tolls and
blessed her road.

### Osbert III, Iron Osbert
*Reigned S.-82 to S.-44*

Built the slag-iron fleet, opened the southern trade and the Round, and spent the Fifth faster than the Citadels could
deliver it. His slag-iron buoys still ring at the Royal Roads.

### Aldric III
*Reigned S.-44 to S.-15*

Paid Osbert's debts by selling offices and raising tolls. In his reign the Slagcrown's assayers first wrote that the
upper seams were thinning (S.-31).

### Aldric IV, the Gentle King
*Crowned S.-15, sailed S.3 · whereabouts unknown*

Crowned young; lost twelve ships in the great storm of S.-7 and spent six years rebuilding the fleet. Called the Gentle
King before S.0 and other things after. A Port Sable factor swears he drowned off the Lazaret Rocks in S.3. The Guest in
Sephar says he is Aldric IV. Osbert, called the Fifth, is said to be his grandson. Nobody knows where he is.

*Sources for the kings: `the-kingdom-and-the-breaking.md`; `the-round-and-the-long-account.md`.*

### Hugh Ardern
*Harbour-marshal of Wexhaven · not recorded after S.3*

**Who.** He led the rear guard that fired the harbour to stop pursuit when the fleet sailed in S.3.
**Hooks.** Gytha Marrin was a customs tallywoman on the quay that night.

*Sources: `the-kingdom-and-the-breaking.md`; the atlas (Wexhaven).*
