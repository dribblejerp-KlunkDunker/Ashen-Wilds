# People: Wexhaven and the Round

The people of The Long Account's voyage, from the Burned Quay to Halsing, as of the thaw of S.30. Each entry ends with
its sources and what was new on 2026-11-19 (ruling O10).

---

### Gytha Marrin, the Receipt-Wife
*Once a customs tallywoman · Wexhaven, the customs vaults · alive, at the cough's crust stage*

**Who.** She was a customs tallywoman at Wexhaven when the fleet sailed. In S.1 and S.2 the Crown took the fen's grain
against paper receipts, *to be repaid in kind, by order of the King*; for twenty-eight years she has taken a receipt
from everyone who walked down the Salt Trace and written them a line in exchange. She holds eleven thousand. She has
ash-lung at the crust stage and cannot go herself. Her commission: *Present them to whoever answers for the Crown. Bring
back what is paid. If nothing is paid, bring back the name of who refused.*
**Looks.** A customs chair on the Burned Quay, a blanket, a tally-board on her knees, the crust at her lips.
**Wants.** An answer, before she stops answering.
**Carries.** The receipts, in barrels. Her ledger of lines written.
**Find her.** The customs vaults; her chair on the quay.
**Hooks.** The last scene of every ending is her chair, with her in it or not.

*Sources: `the-round-and-the-long-account.md`; the atlas (Wexhaven). New: looks.*

### Wat Hebble
*Shipwright · Wexhaven · alive*

**Who.** He spent nine years building the *Small Mercy* from wreck timber, and wants his hull back in one piece.
**Looks.** Adze-scarred shins, a pencil behind each ear, wood shavings in his beard.
**Carries.** Shipwright's tools; caulking and pitch; the *Small Mercy*'s plans scratched on a board.
**Hooks.** He sails with the company or lends the ship; either way he counts every plank on return.

*Sources: `the-round-and-the-long-account.md`. New: looks, carries.*

### Corvin Hale
*Reaver captain · Wexhaven, then Port Sable · alive*

**Who.** His crew is stripping Wexhaven's upper town for stoves. He is licensed, pays his crew, and thinks of himself as
a salvor. He sells a chart of the harbour chains.
**Carries.** A Port Sable licence; the chart of the harbour chains; stove plates in his hold.
**Hooks.** Fight him, trade with him, or sail in his wake and say nothing.

*Sources: `the-round-and-the-long-account.md`; the atlas.*

### Bryde Hallam
*Keeper of the Lazaret Rocks · alive*

**Who.** She was posted for one year, eleven years ago, and wants relief. She keeps the Forty with a handful of keepers.
**Looks.** Salt-cracked hands, a keeper's lantern, a calendar of scratched days on her door.
**Carries.** Clean bills (she can sell one for a stove plate or receipts); the keepers' lime.
**Hooks.** Relieve her, and she will tell you which hulks are sealed and why.

*Sources: `the-round-and-the-long-account.md`; the atlas (the Lazaret Rocks). New: looks.*

### Lucan Daw
*Factor of Daw and Sturry · the Exchange, Port Sable · alive*

**Who.** He wants the receipts at a hundredth of face. If the company sells and sails, grain ships go north, the fen eats
for a winter, and Daw and Sturry hold its land claims.
**Carries.** Ash-paper; Deeps Company shares; a notary bound to him by the bound word.
**Hooks.** Sell and sail.

*Sources: `the-round-and-the-long-account.md`; the atlas.*

### Queen Ysabeau
*Queen of Vauclere · Saint-Aumere · alive*

**Who.** She rules a kingdom of river valleys, terraces and cloth towns whose vines failed in S.2; three Hunger risings
since. She wants the receipts honoured in paper and a writ carried north.
**Carries.** Her chancery's paper, issued in Osbert's name.

*Sources: `the-round-and-the-long-account.md`; the atlas.*

### Osbert, called the Fifth
*At Queen Ysabeau's court · alive · 19*

**Who.** Said to be Aldric IV's grandson, born in exile. Nobody has shown the marriage that would make him so. The
chancery issues paper in his name.

*Sources: `the-round-and-the-long-account.md`.*

### Prior Lambert Strand
*The Keeping in Exile · Saint-Aumere · alive*

**Who.** Abbot Hamon Strand's nephew. He wants silence about a blessing his uncle gave in S.-1. The guest-house he keeps
is a wintering roof in Act II.
**Carries.** The Keeping in Exile's rolls; perhaps Osric Brayne's books, perhaps a Brother Anselm on the rolls of the
returned (see `the-watchhouse.md` and `the-fen.md`).

*Sources: `the-round-and-the-long-account.md`. New: the rolls.*

### The Guest
*An old northerner who says he is Aldric IV · the Garden of Hours, Sephar · alive*

**Who.** He lives as surety for the Debt of Light. A Port Sable factor will swear the King drowned off the Lazaret Rocks
in S.3 and that this is his chaplain. He knows what a king would know; so would a chaplain. He wants to be believed. He
offers to write *I owe this* and sign it, which is worth nothing at the Exchange and is the only thing Gytha asked for.
When the company hands him the Merewick receipt with *Still waiting. S.19.* under it, he asks whose hand that is. The
game never says whether he is Aldric IV.

*Sources: `the-round-and-the-long-account.md`; the atlas (Sephar).*

### Ines Corrant
*Steward of the Deeps Company · Halsing · alive*

**Who.** She wants heat for forty thousand people in the valley towns before another winter. The case for breaking the
wall is hers, made in numbers: last winter's dead are in her ledger by parish. She is not in debt and she is not proud.
She has counted.
**Carries.** The ledger by parish; the Company's charter.

*Sources: `the-round-and-the-long-account.md`; the atlas.*

### Edith Brode
*Spokeswoman of the Low Lodge · Halsing · alive*

**Who.** Ancel Brode's granddaughter. She wants the right of refusal, which no southern law grants. The case against
breaking the wall is hers: unknown ground, wrong air, the right of refusal. Her Lodge is unlawful under the Company's
charter.
**Carries.** Hewers' hand; the Lodge's oath; a lamp she will not carry past the wall.

*Sources: `the-round-and-the-long-account.md`.*
