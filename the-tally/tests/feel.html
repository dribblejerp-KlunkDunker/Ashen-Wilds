Three dummy goblins, a flat yard, and nothing else: the gate is whether the movement is right before anything is built on it. WebGL2 is the default path; ?renderer=canvas is the reference. The resolution stepper is off (quality 1) so both paths draw the same buffer, and ?dpr= sets its cap. F fires the perfect-step reward by hand, because the design gives its reward but not its condition.
